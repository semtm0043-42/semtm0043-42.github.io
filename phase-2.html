Update the
Digital Twin.

A Digital Twin is a model, not an authority. Use the evidence gathered from the real robot to decide which assumptions matter, change them deliberately, and test whether the revised simulation makes better predictions.

Detailed update guidance will be developed here

This page establishes the intended workflow. It will later point to the simulator components and provide practical exercises for revising and testing them.

01—03
Model revision loop

Make assumptions testable

01

Locate the assumption

Read the supplied model and identify where its motor, sensor, geometry, surface, or timing assumptions are represented.

02

Revise from evidence

Use your measurements to justify one change at a time. Record the value, method, and uncertainty rather than treating a fit as unquestionable truth.

03

Predict and compare

Choose motion or sensing data not used to set the model, then compare the new simulation prediction with an independent observation.

Useful disagreement

Mismatch is evidence

When the physical robot and simulation disagree, ask what the mismatch says about the model, measurement method, environment, or trajectory—not which system should be trusted by default.
